### Section 03 & 04: The MECT Difference & The Five Pillars (`InterconnectedSafetyNetSection.tsx`)
- **Intent:** Demonstrate how the 5 interventions form a single mutually reinforcing socio-economic architecture.
- **The Multiplier Principle:**
  1. *Safe Drinking Water (Jal Seva):* Prevents dehydration and waterborne sickness during 45°C heatwaves -> keeps daily-wage parents earning and children in school.
  2. *Food Security & Nutrition (Jan Ahar):* Subsidized hot meals for hospital attendants at Balangir District Hospital -> protects emergency family savings from depletion.
  3. *Women's Vocational Livelihoods (Tailoring):* Multi-month certified courses -> maternal cash flow directly funds daughters' education and prevents early child marriage.
  4. *Remedial & Higher Education:* Village study hubs + direct accredited college fee grants -> converts first-generation learners into certified professionals.
  5. *Community Welfare & Civic Rights:* Winter blankets, disaster aid, and grassroots legal literacy -> shields families from 120% APR predatory moneylenders during crises.
- **Interactive Systemic Navigator:** Clean tabbed inspection panel with Problem, MECT Intervention, Multi-Dimensional Impact, and Verifiable Field Evidence for each pillar.

## 1. The Five Essential Questions

To establish genuine institutional authority, the website must answer five questions naturally and compellingly:

### Question 1: Why does MECT exist?
- **Root Context:** Western Odisha (Balangir, Subarnapur, Nuapada, Kalahandi corridor) has historically faced high agrarian vulnerability, seasonal distress migration, low female workforce participation, and steep educational drop-out rates among first-generation learners.
- **Institutional Raison d'Être:** Founded on 27 July 2013 (Registered Trust Deed No. 40311302194, Balangir Sub-Registrar), MECT exists not as a remote charity, but as a permanent, locally rooted community safety net dedicated to breaking generational cycles of rural poverty through education, self-reliance, and basic human dignity.

### Question 2: What specific problems does MECT work to solve?
- **Extreme Heat & Water Scarcity:** Summer temperatures exceeding 45°C in Balangir cause acute gastrointestinal disease, waterborne morbidity, and medical debt.
- **Hospital Attendant Hunger:** At Balangir District Hospital, impoverished family members caring for admitted patients routinely skip meals to preserve funds for medicines.
- **Female Economic Dependency:** Rural women lack independent vocational earning avenues, leaving households completely exposed to single-earner agricultural crop failures.
- **Vernacular School Dropout Rates:** Rural first-generation students in vernacular medium schools fail Board examinations and drop out before college due to lack of after-school academic coaching and college fees.
- **Catastrophic Debt Traps:** Winter cold waves, flash floods, or acute family emergencies force agrarian families to borrow from informal moneylenders at 60–120% APR, forfeiting land or productive assets.

### Question 3: How does MECT approach those problems differently?
- **The Interconnected Safety Net:** MECT does not run five disconnected charities. It recognizes that clean water protects children's health; health keeps them in school; mother's tailoring income funds schooling supplies; remedial hubs help them clear Board exams; and community welfare protects the family from asset loss.
- **100% Honorary Leadership (Clause XVI):** Every trustee serves without salary, sitting fee, honorarium, or per diem reimbursement under registered Trust Deed Clause XVI.
- **Direct Institutional Disbursal:** 100% of educational tuition grants are disbursed directly to verified accredited college and university accounts via electronic NEFT/RTGS channels with zero intermediary deductions.
- **Dual-Fund Financial Segregation:** Contributions for the proposed Grand Hanuman Murti cultural landmark are segregated under Sub-Ledger `HANUMAN_MURTI`, never commingled with educational or welfare funds.

### Question 4: What evidence demonstrates its work and accountability?
- **Audited Telemetry (Activity Logs):**
  - **350+ Student Participation Entries** (FY 2023–24 Activity Record across rural Balangir remedial centers).
  - **120+ Cumulative Vocational Course Enrolments** (FY 2021–23 multi-cohort cycles in tailoring and craft fabrication).
  - **10,000+ Water and Relief Service Instances** (Multi-year field service log across seasonal Jal Seva kiosks and hospital corridors).
  - **13+ Years Continuous Trust Tenure** (Estd. 27 July 2013).
- **Explicit Target Qualification:** The 200-student higher education target is strictly labeled as a *Proposed Target* (confirmed funded places currently stand at zero until formal board sanction and intake announcement).
- **Statutory Registry:** Fully published PAN (`AADTM5474F`), 12A URN (`AADTM5474FE20231`), 80G URN (`AADTM5474FF20241`), MCA CSR-1 (`CSR00081063`), and NITI Aayog NGO Darpan (`OR/2024/0466491`).
- **Physical Public Inspection:** Open inspection of statutory records at the Registered Office in Rugudipara, Balangir by appointment.

### Question 5: How can visitors meaningfully participate in its mission?
- **Six Clear Pathways:**
  1. *Individual Donors:* Direct giving with instant digital receipts and 80G tax certification workflow.
  2. *Academic Volunteers & Mentors:* Direct doubt-clearing tutoring, career guidance, and field fellowship.
  3. *Corporate CSR Partners:* Formal Schedule VII compliance, turnkey project execution, milestone-based disbursements, and Utilization Certificates.
  4. *Educational Institutions:* Partnering with accredited colleges and schools for direct student fee remittances and student identification.
  5. *Community Supporters:* In-kind distributions (winter blankets, books, tailoring kits) and localized volunteer coordination.
  6. *Institutional & Statutory Collaborators:* Administrative alignment with district authorities, legal aid clinics, and community healthcare workers.

## 3. Ethical Storytelling & Safeguarding Standards

1. **Dignity & Respect:** Never depict rural citizens as passive victims. Showcase them as active agents—mothers mastering tailoring machinery, students solving mathematics, elders accessing dignified hydration.
2. **Child Protection:** In strict compliance with the Juvenile Justice Act and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDPA), no student's home address, school roll number, or family financial record is published online.
3. **Verified Photographic Provenance:** All hero and archival imagery is sourced directly from verified Balangir operational sessions or formally attributed Creative Commons sources.
4. **Honest Metric Reporting:** Distinguish cumulative service instances from unique individuals served; distinguish confirmed historical activities from proposed prospective targets.

## 1. Executive Summary & Transformation Overview

MECT Phase 26 achieves a **comprehensive frontend visual reinvention**, elevating the public digital identity from a conventional mid-2010s NGO card template into a **globally credible, documentary-grade cultural institution**.

The new visual identity — **"Documentary Editorial Architecture"** — synthesizes:
1. **The physical soil, warm laterite bricks, and archival parchment of Western Odisha** (sunbaked terracotta `#B85D3B`, deep carbon basalt ink `#0F1714`, archival alabaster `#FAF7F2`, and razor hairline borders `#E7E0D5`).
2. **The editorial discipline of international cultural publications and museums** (asymmetrical 12-column grids, high-contrast literary serif headlines, museum-grade documentary captions, tactile micro-borders, and spatial interactive navigators).

All existing institutional facts, statutory registrations, ring-fenced financial sub-ledgers (`HANUMAN_MURTI`), and technical workflows (Cashfree, Razorpay, UPI, manual bank transfer, PDF receipts, donor portal) have been **100% preserved**.

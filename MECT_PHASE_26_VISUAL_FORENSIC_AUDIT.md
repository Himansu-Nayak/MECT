## 3. Viewport & Mobile Deficiencies Audit

### 3.1 Mobile Viewports (390px & 430px)
- **Problem:** On mobile screens, the previous design squeezed multi-column elements into repetitive vertical card stacks that required excessive scrolling (over 12,000px of page length).
- **Hero Stacking:** Centered text pushed all visual imagery below the fold, resulting in a wall of dark text on mobile load.
- **Touch Targets:** Navigation links and micro-buttons had cramped tap boundaries (< 44px), risking accidental taps.
- **Direction:** Mobile-first editorial layouts featuring full-width bleed imagery, punchy headline typography (`clamp(2rem, 8vw, 2.75rem)`), fluid vertical spacing, and touch-optimized horizontal carousel scrolls for dense data.

### 3.2 Tablet & Small Laptop (768px & 1024px)
- **Problem:** Awkward 2-column wraps where 3 cards were intended, leaving orphan cards with vast empty whitespace.
- **Direction:** Purpose-built 12-column responsive grid rules with graceful 8-column editorial column widths and sticky contextual sidebars.

### 3.3 Large Desktop (1440px & 1920px)
- **Problem:** Content stretched unnaturally wide or stayed trapped in narrow 1280px containers with vast grey borders.
- **Direction:** Generous 1440px/1600px architectural canvas with asymmetric margins, full-bleed documentary photo ribbons, and balanced negative space.
